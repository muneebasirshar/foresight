Questions

Straight answers.

How does Cadence learn my voice?

From your own words. Upload past session transcripts and Cadence keeps only the lines you spoke, building a private tone profile: how you open, how long your sentences run, the phrases you reach for. You can also paste in a few past LinkedIn posts so it learns how you write, not just how you talk. Every new session makes the profile sharper.

What do I need to get started?

A Word (.docx) transcript with speaker labels, which most meeting and webinar tools can export. One session is enough to begin; five to fifteen gives the best results.

Will it post to LinkedIn for me?

No, and that’s deliberate. Cadence drafts, you decide. Every post is yours to edit, regenerate or discard, and you publish it yourself.

What do I get from each session?

Up to three standout moments, each with a short reason it’s worth sharing, and up to two drafts: an insight post and an encouragement post. If a session doesn’t contain three strong moments, you’ll get fewer rather than filler.

Is my data private?

Each presenter’s transcripts, tone profile and posts are isolated from every other presenter’s, enforced at the database level. Transcripts are processed by an AI model to produce drafts. Read the privacy page for specifics, and only upload sessions you have the right to use.

Can I use client workshops?

Only with your client’s permission. Cadence works on whatever you give it, so confidentiality is your call. Many people start with their own talks, webinars and public sessions.

What does it cost?

Free while we're in private beta. We're onboarding a small founding cohort personally. Pricing will be announced before anything is charged, and early members will hear first.

Which formats and platforms are supported?

Right now: .docx transcripts in, LinkedIn posts out. Other formats and platforms are on the list, and the early cohort helps decide the order.

Privacy

Plain-language privacy

This page covers the early-access sign-up on this website. It is a summary written for people, not a substitute for legal advice.

What we collect

When you request early access we store the email address you give us. We also record which page and campaign link brought you here (for example, UTM parameters) so we know which channels are worth our time.

What we do with it

We use it to contact you about your early access and to decide who to invite first. We don’t sell your details and we don’t send marketing unrelated to Cadence.

Spam protection

To limit abuse we keep a one-way hash of your network address, never the address itself, and use it only to rate-limit sign-ups.

Where it lives

Sign-up data is stored in a private database that the public website cannot read. Only the Cadence team can access it.

If you become a user

Transcripts you upload are processed by an AI model to build your tone profile and draft posts, and are isolated from every other presenter’s data. Only upload sessions you have the right to use. You’ll get fuller terms before you upload anything.

Your choices

Reply to any email from us and ask to be removed, and we’ll delete your sign-up details.
